### 4.2 Detailed service descriptions

> Intro: We deliver advanced fire protection systems that are engineered to detect, control and suppress fire hazards effectively. Our solutions ensure safety of people, property and operations across all types of facilities.

#### Fire Hydrant Systems
Our fire hydrant systems are designed to ensure immediate and reliable water availability during fire emergencies. We provide complete solutions including design, supply, installation, testing and commissioning of **hydrant pumps, underground and overhead piping networks, landing valves, hose reels, hose cabinets, yard hydrants** and all associated accessories. Engineered as per **NBC, IS and NFPA** standards, our systems deliver optimum pressure, strong water flow and wide coverage to protect industrial plants, commercial buildings, high-rise structures, warehouses, institutions and other critical facilities with long-term reliability and effective performance.

#### Fire Sprinkler Systems
We offer high-performance fire sprinkler systems that automatically detect heat and discharge water to control or suppress fire at the earliest stage. Our solutions include **Wet Pipe, Dry Pipe, Pre-action, Deluge and ESFR** sprinkler systems with accurate hydraulic design, high-quality pipes, valves and accessories. All systems are installed and tested as per **NFPA 13, IS standards** and other applicable codes to ensure uniform water distribution, quick activation and minimum damage to life and property. These systems are ideal for factories, commercial complexes, hospitals, malls, data centers, hotels, cold storages, airports and any facility requiring automatic fire suppression.

#### Fire Alarm & Detection Systems
Our intelligent fire alarm and detection systems provide early warning, quick response and safe evacuation by identifying smoke, heat, flame and gas at the initial stage. We supply and install **addressable and conventional fire alarm panels, smoke, heat, flame and gas detectors, manual call points, sounders, beacons, voice evacuation systems** and integration with other safety systems. Designed for accuracy and reliability, our systems ensure real-time alarm notification and monitoring, helping protect occupants and assets in commercial, industrial, institutional and high-risk environments.

#### Fire Extinguisher Services
We provide complete fire extinguisher solutions including supply, installation, refilling, maintenance and annual servicing of all types of portable and wheeled extinguishers such as **ABC Dry Powder, CO₂, Foam, Water, Wet Chemical and Clean Agent** extinguishers. Our team assesses the risk profile of your premises and recommends the right type and capacity of extinguishers as per **NBC and IS** standards. We ensure proper placement, clear signage, regular inspection, hydrostatic testing and replacement to keep the equipment in perfect working condition. Our goal is to ensure that the first line of fire defense is always ready, reliable and easy to use during any emergency, providing maximum safety to occupants and property.

#### Fire NOC Services
We assist clients in obtaining **Fire NOC from Local Fire Authorities, Municipal Corporations and other statutory bodies**. Our services include documentation, drawing preparation, system design review, site inspection coordination, compliance verification and liaison with authorities till final approval. We ensure that all fire protection systems, equipment and building requirements are in compliance with **NBC, State Fire Act** and local fire safety regulations. With our expertise and strong coordination, we make the NOC process smooth, transparent and time-efficient, enabling your project or building to operate legally and without any interruption.

#### Fire Design & Engineering
We provide professional fire protection design and engineering services for all types of facilities including industrial, commercial, residential, institutional and high-rise buildings. Our services include **risk assessment, system designing, hydraulic calculations, equipment selection, BOQ preparation, technical specifications and detailed drawing preparation**. We design systems such as fire hydrant, sprinkler, foam system, deluge system, fire alarm and detection system as per **NBC, IS, NFPA** and international standards. Our engineered solutions ensure optimal performance, safety, compliance and cost efficiency throughout the lifecycle of the project.
